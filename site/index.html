01 / The keyboard

Less keyboard.
More flow.

The Wolf is a compact, column-staggered split inspired by a simple idea: keep the keys you use close, and give your hands room to rest. Each half has 20 keys, including a three-key splayed thumb cluster.

It pairs a XIAO nRF52840 wireless controller with MX-compatible switches, a slim aluminum case, white backlighting, and subtle RGB status lights.
